# OramiVoice Documentation & User Tutorials

OramiVoice is an all-in-one AI production platform designed for multilingual voice synthesis, audiobook dramatization, digital book publishing, creative visuals, whiteboard animations, automated video editing, audio-video translation, and browser-based audio engineering.

---

## Workspace Navigation & Module Directory

The platform navigation is structured into distinct functional production suites:

### 1. Text to Speech
- **[Voice Studio](guide/text-to-speech.md)**: High-fidelity speech synthesis supporting multi-lingual narration, speed/pitch modulation, and custom voice models.
- **[Subtitle Dubbing](guide/dubbing.md)**: Synchronized voiceover generation from subtitle files (SRT, VTT) with automated pacing optimization.
- **[Voice Library](guide/voices.md)**: Filterable catalog categorized by gender, age, language, accent, and style.
- **[Create New Voice](guide/voice-cloning.md)**: Instant zero-shot voice cloning from audio sample recordings.
- **[SSML & Non-Verbal Tags](guide/ssml-guide.md)**: Expressive vocal control using inline tags (`[laughter]`, `[sigh]`, `[surprise-oh]`, etc.) and SSML tags (`<break>`, `<prosody>`, `<whisper>`).

### 2. AI Novel & Audiobook
- **[Idea Lab](guide/novel-ideas.md)**: Story concept brainstormer, commercial genre tropes, and multi-act plot outline generator.
- **[Bookshelf](guide/novel-bookshelf.md)**: Multi-project management hub with word counts, audio duration metrics, and cover artwork.
- **[Novel Studio](guide/novel-studio.md)**: Horizontal chapter rail, manuscript splitter, and audio staging editor.
- **[Character Bible & Casting](guide/novel-characters.md)**: Persistent character memory, dialogue speaker tagging, and multi-voice casting.
- **[Digital Book Publishing](guide/novel-publishing.md)**: WYSIWYG Print PDF preview (with 25+ book typefaces), EPUB e-book export, Amazon KDP formats, and book cover design.

### 3. AI Creative Studio
- **[Auto Edit Video Studio](guide/auto-edit-studio.md)**: Non-linear CapCut-style multi-track video timeline, 1:1 voiceover auto-sync, Ken Burns camera motions, and client-side GPU MP4 rendering.
- **[Whiteboard Video](guide/whiteboard-video.md)**: Animated hand-drawn whiteboard explainer studio with pencil/marker/stylus tools, 12 reveal styles, and multi-track audio sync.
- **[AI Image Studio](guide/image-studio.md)**: Single and batch image rendering, multi-reference conditioning, 5 aspect ratios, and clean downloads.
- **[Prompt Engineering](guide/prompt-engineering.md)**: Visual styling guide, lighting tokens, and camera compositions.

### 4. Translation
- **[Translate Subtitles](guide/translate-subtitles.md)**: Bilingual side-by-side subtitle editor with timestamp preservation.
- **[Translate Audio](guide/translate-audio.md)**: Voice transcription, neural translation, and synthesized voiceover master tracks.
- **[Translate Video](guide/translate-video.md)**: Video localization with voiceover replacement and optional burned-in subtitles.

### 5. Voice & Video Studio
- **[Speech to Text](guide/speech-to-text.md)**: Accurate multilingual transcription with exportable text, SRT, and VTT files.
- **[Video Maker](guide/video-maker.md)**: Dynamic waveform audiograms and animated captions for TikTok, Shorts, and Reels.
- **[Voice Changer](guide/voice-changer.md)**: Timbre transformation preserving natural speech timing and inflection.
- **[Desktop Client (Windows)](guide/desktop-app.md)**: Native desktop application with microphone calibration, offline voice synthesis, and local RTX GPU acceleration.

### 6. Free Audio Tools
- **[Audio Tools Suite](guide/audio-tools.md)**: 20 browser-based client-side audio utilities executed via Web Audio API and WebAssembly.

### 7. Infrastructure, Plans & Developer Reference
- **[Subscription, Plans & Credits](guide/pricing.md)**: Starter $17 (500K credits), Unlimited Neural Engine (OTO 1), and Unlimited Image tiers.
- **[Self-Hosted GPU & Colab Integration](guide/self-hosted-gpu.md)**: Connect Google Colab, RunPod, or local NVIDIA RTX GPUs for zero-credit synthesis.
- **[History & Exports](guide/history.md)**: Consolidated asset management and project downloads.
- **[API Overview & Speech/Image APIs](guide/api-reference.md)**: RESTful developer integration endpoints.
