# History & Asset Management

The History and Asset Manager (`/history.php`) serves as the central activity log and media archive for all generation tasks executed across your workspace.

---

## Log Categories

You can filter and review production history across multiple categories:

- **Voice Synthesis Logs**: Tracks script text, voice model used, character count, duration, audio preview, and download links.
- **Image Generation Logs**: Records visual prompts, models (`Nano Banana Pro` / `Nano Banana`), aspect ratios, and full-resolution images.
- **Video Dubbing Logs**: Archive of transcribed video projects and localized video downloads.
- **Novel Studio Projects**: Multi-chapter video compositions and story drafts.

---

## Asset Retrieval & Search

- **Date Range Filtering**: Filter generation history by Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, or custom date ranges.
- **Search by Prompt / Text**: Search across all past script texts and visual prompts.
- **Re-Download & Remix**: Re-download original assets or reuse prompt parameters with one click.
