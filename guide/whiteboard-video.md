# Whiteboard Video Studio

The **Whiteboard Video Studio** (`/media.php`) is a studio-grade production workspace designed for creating animated hand-drawn whiteboard explainer videos, sketch storytelling, and educational presentations.

---

## Core Features & Capabilities

- **Natural Hand-Drawn Animation**: Simulates authentic human hand gestures and sketching motions drawing artwork directly onto the whiteboard in real time.
- **Multiple Tool Models**: Choose between authentic drawing instruments:
  - 🖊️ **Whiteboard Marker**: Classic bold felt-tip marker on smooth whiteboard surfaces.
  - ✏️ **Wooden Pencil**: Crisp graphite pencil strokes with authentic sketch textures.
  - ✒️ **Technical Fineliner**: Ultra-sharp architectural ink line tracing.
  - 🪄 **Digital Stylus**: Sleek minimalist tablet pen for modern vector graphics.
  - 🖌️ **Watercolor Brush**: Artistic color wash strokes.
  - 📁 **Custom Hand**: Upload your own branded hand graphic (PNG with alpha channel).
- **Hand Orientation & Scale**:
  - **Hand Side**: Switch between **Right Hand** and **Left Hand** for authentic presentation perspective.
  - **Hand Scale**: Adjust hand tool scale between Small (38%), Normal (46%), and Large (55%).
- **12 Dynamic Color Reveal Styles**:
  - `🎲 Dynamic Random (Auto)`: Automatically alternates color fill paths for maximum visual dynamism.
  - `🎨 Regional Patch Paint`: Fills colors area-by-area as if painted by an artist.
  - `📐 Diagonal 45° Down / Up`: Clean angular wipes across the canvas.
  - `🌀 Spiral Bloom (Out) / Spiral Inward`: Radiates colors outwards or draws inward from borders.
  - `⬇️ Top-Down / ⬆️ Bottom-Up Sweep`: Linear reveal along vertical axes.
  - `➡️ Left-to-Right / ⬅️ Right-to-Left Wipe`: Classic text and story reading flow.
  - `🔆 Radial Iris Out`: Expands from the center point outward.
  - `🌊 Watercolor Fade`: Soft gradient diffusion into final colors.
- **Auto-Key Background Removal**: Instantly removes solid white or pale backgrounds from uploaded images, creating clean transparent lineart that sits naturally on any board texture.
- **Background Textures**: Select from *Clean Whiteboard*, *Warm Parchment*, *Dark Chalkboard*, *Blueprint Grid*, *Ruled Notebook*, *Dot Grid Paper*, *Vintage Linen*, *Warm Wood Desk*, or upload custom wallpapers.
- **CapCut Multi-Track Timeline**:
  - **Filmstrip Image Previews**: Real-time thumbnail previews repeating across each scene block.
  - **Right-Edge Resize Handles**: Drag right edge to adjust scene durations with live second tooltips.
  - **Drag & Drop Reordering**: Rearrange scenes along the timeline with visual insertion indicators.
  - **Audio & Subtitle Sync**: Automatic alignment with voiceover narration and timestamped captions.
- **Collapsible Upload Panel**: Click the <kbd>↕</kbd> toggle to collapse the drop area or drag the bottom edge to resize, giving full visibility to studio controls on any display.

## Step-by-Step Production Guide

### Step 1: Ingest Assets
1. Open **Whiteboard Video** (`/media.php`).
2. Drag and drop illustrations, concept sketches, or Notion-style graphics into **1. Batch Images**.
3. Use the toggle button (<kbd>↕</kbd>) to collapse the drop area into a sleek mini bar if you want more room for controls.

### Step 2: Add Voiceover & Subtitles
1. Drop your narration audio (`.mp3` or `.wav`) into **2. Audio & Subtitles**.
2. Optionally attach a `.srt` or `.vtt` subtitle file.
3. Click **Auto-Fit Audio** on the timeline to automatically match slide durations to spoken lines.

### Step 3: Configure Artistic Hand & Revealing Effects
1. Select your preferred **Drawing Hand** tool (e.g. Whiteboard Marker or Wooden Pencil).
2. Choose **Hand Side** (Right or Left handed) and scale.
3. Select your **Reveal Style** (Dynamic Random, Spiral Bloom, or Regional Patch Paint).
4. Toggle **Auto-Key BG** to transparentize white image backgrounds automatically.

### Step 4: Preview & Export
1. Scrub the timeline playhead or click Play to preview the realistic drawing animation synchronized with voiceover.
2. Click **Render & Export Video** to produce a Full HD 1080p MP4 file.
