# AI Audio & Video Translation Suite

The Translation Suite (`/translate.php`) offers complete multi-lingual localization across three dedicated modes: **Translate Subtitles**, **Translate Audio**, and **Translate Video**.

---

## 1. Translate Subtitles (`/translate.php?mode=subtitles`)

- **Bilingual Side-by-Side Editor**: Upload `.srt` or `.vtt` files and translate dialogue lines into over 30 global languages with instant side-by-side comparison.
- **Context-Aware Translation**: Translates complete conversational context rather than isolated words, ensuring natural tone and cultural accuracy.
- **Timing Preservation**: Retains exact start and end millisecond timestamps for all subtitle blocks.
- **Direct Subtitle Export**: Download translated `.srt` and `.vtt` files ready for media players and streaming platforms.

---

## 2. Translate Audio (`/translate.php?mode=audio`)

- **Spoken Audio Translation**: Upload foreign language speech recordings (podcasts, interviews, voice memos).
- **Automated Workflow**: Transcribes the source audio, translates the manuscript into the chosen target language, and synthesizes localized speech with selected voices.
- **Master Audio Download**: Export the localized audio file in WAV or MP3 format.

---

## 3. Translate Video (`/translate.php?mode=video`)

- **Video Localization**: Upload video files (MP4, MOV, WebM) for full multi-lingual translation.
- **Multi-Track Processing**: Separates spoken dialogue, translates subtitles, generates synchronized voiceovers, and reconstructs the audio track with preserved background acoustics.
- **Video Export**: Download localized videos with optional burned-in bilingual subtitles.

---

## Supported Languages

The engine supports full bidirectional translation between 34+ languages, including Vietnamese, English (US/UK), Mandarin Chinese, Cantonese, Taiwanese Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, Spanish (Spain/LatAm), French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Portugal/Brazil), Russian, Thai, Indonesian, Arabic, Hindi, Bengali, Dutch, Turkish, Polish, Ukrainian, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Finnish, Greek, Czech, Hungarian, Romanian, Filipino, and Malay.
