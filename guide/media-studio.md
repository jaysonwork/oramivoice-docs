# Speech & Video Studio Suite

The Studio Suite (`/studio.php`) is a multi-modal audio-visual workstation providing three dedicated production workflows: **Speech to Text**, **Video Maker (Audiogram)**, and **Voice Changer**.

---

## 1. Speech to Text (`/studio.php?mode=transcribe`)

Converts recorded audio or video files into accurate, timestamped text transcripts and exportable subtitle files.

- **Audio & Video Ingestion**: Supports MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4, WebM, and MOV formats.
- **Multilingual Recognition**: Automatically detects and transcribes over 30 languages.
- **Timestamped Transcripts**: Generates segment-level and word-level timestamps.
- **Export Options**: Download transcripts as plain text (`.txt`), SRT subtitles (`.srt`), or WebVTT captions (`.vtt`).

---

## 2. Video Maker / Audiograms (`/studio.php?mode=video-maker`)

Transforms podcast clips, voiceovers, and speech audio into engaging social media videos with dynamic waveforms and animated subtitles.

- **Canvas Formats**: Vertical 9:16 (TikTok, Shorts, Reels), Square 1:1 (Instagram, Feed), and Landscape 16:9 (YouTube, Web).
- **Audio Waveforms**: Dynamic animated waveforms, soundbars, circular sound visualizers, and frequency bars that react to your voice track.
- **Animated Captions**: Word-by-word karaoke highlighting, custom typography, font sizing, and highlight color schemes.
- **Background & Cover Art**: Upload custom background images, apply color gradient fills, or import visuals generated in Image Studio.
- **Direct Video Export**: Renders Full HD MP4 video files ready for social sharing.

---

## 3. Voice Changer (`/studio.php?mode=voice-changer`)

Re-voices recorded speech files into any selected persona from the Voice Library while maintaining original intonation and timing.

- Upload any vocal sample or speech recording.
- Choose a target actor voice from the Voice Catalog.
- The engine replaces the vocal timbre while matching the cadence and pacing of the original speaker.
