# Voice Changer

The Voice Changer (`/studio.php?mode=voice-changer`) converts recorded speech audio into another voice persona while preserving natural speech rhythm, inflection, and cadence.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **Timbre Transformation**: Replaces the vocal tone of an uploaded recording with any selected character or narrator voice from the Voice Catalog.
- **Cadence Preservation**: Maintains the emotional emphasis, speech speed, pauses, and expressive timing of the original speaker.
- **Noise Suppression**: Cleans input audio before voice conversion for crisp vocal output.

---

## Operating Steps

1. Navigate to **Voice Changer** (`/studio.php?mode=voice-changer`).
2. Upload your source vocal recording (WAV, MP3, M4A) or record directly using the microphone.
3. Select your target persona from the Voice Library.
4. Click **Transform Voice**.
5. Listen to the re-voiced preview and click **Download Audio**.
