# OramiVoice Desktop Client

The **OramiVoice Desktop Application** brings ultra-low-latency voice synthesis, microphone cloning, and local batch processing directly to your Windows workstation.

---

## Why Use the Desktop App?

- **Zero-Latency Audio Preview**: Scrub and preview voice synthesis directly through native Windows WASAPI audio buffers.
- **Local Microphone Live Cloning**: Plug in your studio microphone and record calibration samples directly into the local voice engine without browser permissions or cloud uploads.
- **Offline & Local GPU Processing**: Run lightweight neural models on your local NVIDIA RTX graphics card when working offline.
- **Deep File System Integration**: Drag and drop entire folders of audio, scripts, or video clips for batch generation.

---

## Installation & Account Pairing

### Step 1: Download the Desktop Client
1. In the web portal, navigate to [Account Settings -> Desktop App](/profile.php?tab=desktop).
2. Click **Download Desktop Client for Windows (.exe)**.
3. Run the installer and launch OramiVoice.

### Step 2: Pair with Your OramiVoice Account
1. On the **Desktop App** tab in your profile, find your unique **Desktop App Pairing Key**.
2. Click **Copy Key**.
3. Open the desktop client, paste the key into the login prompt, and click **Pair Device**.
4. Your account credits, custom voices, audiobooks, and templates will synchronize automatically.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Studio Mic Calibration
- Use the built-in **Acoustic Calibrator** to measure background room noise and set ideal input gain.
- Record a 5-second clean speech sample to generate high-fidelity instant voice clones.

### 2. Batch Media & Export Queue
- Process multi-chapter audiobooks or hundreds of video narration clips in parallel.
- Export directly to WAV (24-bit 48kHz), MP3 (320kbps), or FLAC lossless formats.

### 3. Automatic Updates
- The desktop client automatically checks for engine improvements, new voice models, and performance optimizations upon startup.
