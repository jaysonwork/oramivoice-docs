# Speech to Text

The Speech to Text studio (`/studio.php?mode=transcribe`) transcribes spoken audio and video recordings into text transcripts and timestamped subtitle files.

![Voice & Video Studio](assets/images/ui_video_studio.png)

---

## Supported Media Formats

- **Audio**: MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, AAC, OGG, WebM.
- **Video**: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM.

---

## Core Capabilities

- **High-Accuracy Transcription**: Transcribes spoken dialogue across more than 30 supported languages with automated punctuation and sentence casing.
- **Segment & Word Timestamps**: Produces millisecond-accurate timecodes suitable for subtitle editing and video synchronization.
- **Multi-Format Export**:
  - Plain Text (`.txt`) for meeting minutes and article drafts.
  - SubRip Subtitles (`.srt`) for video players and editing timelines.
  - WebVTT (`.vtt`) for web video players and HTML5 streaming.

---

## How to Transcribe Audio or Video

1. Navigate to **Speech to Text** (`/studio.php?mode=transcribe`).
2. Upload your audio or video file.
3. Select the spoken language (or leave on Auto-Detect).
4. Click **Start Transcription**.
5. Review the generated transcript in the interactive editor.
6. Click **Download** and choose your preferred output format (`.txt`, `.srt`, or `.vtt`).
